BasketMap helps people in Lyon and Paris build a shopping list, see which
products can genuinely be compared across nearby stores, and understand which
items are available only at a specific supermarket.

The product uses source-linked price observations rather than invented demo
prices. It keeps incomplete coverage visible, saves a returning visitor's
basket in the browser, and avoids recommending a store from a partial total.
